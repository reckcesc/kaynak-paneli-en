Sustainable Resource Management Panel

Air Quality, Water Quality and Waste monitoring in a single panel — Student Projects
📊 Project completed in 2025

This panel is a multi-module data console designed to let air quality, water quality, and waste data across campus be entered and monitored from one place. Select a measurement point (building), a measurement type and a value, save it, and the data appears instantly in the table and summary cards below.

⚠️ Demo mode: This panel runs entirely in the browser (client-side) to demonstrate the project's planned data model — it is not connected to a central database. Data you enter is only visible while this page is open and resets when the page is refreshed.
